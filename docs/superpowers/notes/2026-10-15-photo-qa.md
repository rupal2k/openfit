# Photo logging: device QA (Task 9)

**Date:** 2026-10-15
**Build:** `feat/photo-logging` @ `97e4710`
**Model:** Food-101 classifier, WASM int8 (93 MB) plus the ONNX Runtime wasm (27 MB) from jsDelivr.
**How it was tested:**
- The APK's WebView on the S21 FE was driven over Chrome DevTools (`adb forward` to `webview_devtools_remote_<pid>`).
- Desktop runs used headless Chromium.
- The test photos were the spike's set (Food-101 and Wikimedia Commons images, kept locally).

**Phone:** Samsung Galaxy S21 FE (SM-G990B2), Android 16, WebView Chrome 154. WebGPU is present, deviceMemory 8, not cross-origin isolated.
